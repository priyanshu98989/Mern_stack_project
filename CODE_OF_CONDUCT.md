# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in CertTrack to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Do not publish private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption and
publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions or ban accounts
that violate this code. Report incidents privately to the maintainers.
